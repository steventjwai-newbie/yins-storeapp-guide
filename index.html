How to use the app

From the home screen, users choose Dine in, Reservation, Pick Up, or Delivery.用户可在主页选择堂食、预约、到店自取或外送服务。

Dine in

Select a store, browse the menu, add items, then choose table number and payment method.选择门店，浏览菜单并加入商品，然后选择桌号和付款方式。

Delivery

Enter delivery location, confirm items, choose delivery time, and complete address details.输入外送地点，确认商品，选择送达时间，并填写完整地址。

Reservation

Select store, guest count, booking date and time, and add remarks if needed.选择门店、人数、预约日期和时间，如有需要可填写备注。

Table QR

Scan the table QR to link the order to the restaurant location and table number.扫描桌号二维码，即可将订单连接到对应的餐厅地点和桌号。

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for existing members, discounts, app issues, and the difference between redeemable points and tier progress.以下是旧会员、折扣、App 问题，以及可兑换积分与会员等级积分差异的常见问题说明。

How do old members retrieve their points?

Old members from the previous Yin's app or FeedMe membership system should register with their previously registered email and phone number to support a smooth points transition.旧 Yin's App 或 FeedMe 会员系统的旧会员，请使用之前注册的电邮和电话号码重新注册，以便积分顺利转移。

Can members still use corporate or resident discounts?

No. Customers may use either in-store discounts or app member benefits, but not both at the same time.不可以。顾客只能选择店内折扣或 App 会员福利其中一种，不能同时享有两项优惠。

Who should customers contact for app issues?

If customers have app-related issues, please leave their registered phone number, email address, and name. Our HQ will check accordingly within 3-5 business days.如顾客遇到 App 相关问题，请留下注册电话号码、电邮地址和姓名。总部将会在 3-5 个工作日内进行查询与处理。

What is the difference between Points and Accumulated Points?

Points are the redeemable balance customers can spend on vouchers or rewards. They include points from purchases, daily check-ins, and rewards. Accumulated Points are used for membership tier progress and count only points earned from actual purchases.Points 是可兑换余额，可用于兑换优惠券或奖励，来源包括消费、每日签到和奖励。Accumulated Points 则用于会员等级进度，只计算实际消费所获得的积分。

How long does my tier last?

Your membership tier is checked using a rolling 2-month window: the current month plus the previous month. Think of it like a moving basket. Every day, the app looks at the valid Accumulated Points in this basket to decide your tier.您的会员等级会根据滚动 2 个月计算，即当前月份加上上一个月份。可以把它想成一个会移动的积分篮子：App 会查看这个篮子里的有效 Accumulated Points 来决定您的会员等级。

Each tier has its own required Accumulated Points level. If the points in your current 2-month basket stay within a tier's requirement, you remain in that tier. If the total drops below that tier's requirement, your tier may be adjusted based on the points that are still valid in the basket.每个会员等级都有各自需要达到的 Accumulated Points。只要您当前 2 个月积分篮子里的积分仍符合该等级要求，您就会保留在该等级。如果总积分低于该等级要求，会员等级可能会根据篮子里仍然有效的积分进行调整。
